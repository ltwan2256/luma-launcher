Windows build of the pinned Orbital Horizon fork with LUMA's local skin rendering patch and Orbital Laser 0.5.3.

This is a technical preview, not the finished LUMA Mods catalog update. Multiplayer effect synchronization and rejection of incompatible mod configurations still require integration and end-to-end tests. The catalog manifest deliberately has `catalogEnabled: false` and `multiplayerReady: false`.

The ZIP contains only the client, mod, checksums and licenses. Minecraft resources, personal settings and worlds are excluded.

Horizon fork: https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/tree/cbceb3f0e8dbd2eba25d3ba0c826338c93e35226

Orbital Laser by Stink-O: https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser/releases/tag/v0.5.3
